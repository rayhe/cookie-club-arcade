How to Play

  • Move the paddle with your mouse, your finger, or the arrow keys.
  • Tap or press Space to launch the star ball.
  • Every block you break scores 10 points. Clear them all to reach the next level!
  • If the ball slips past your paddle, you lose one of your 3 hearts ❤️.
