How to Play

  • Steer your ship with the arrow keys, your mouse, or your finger.
  • Press Space, tap, or use the ZAP! button to fire a sparkle beam.
  • Every meteor you zap scores 10 points. Big meteors split into two little ones!
  • If a meteor lands, it bonks one of your 3 shields 🛡️. Protect the planet!
