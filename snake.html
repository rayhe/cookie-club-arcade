How to Play

  • Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, a swipe, or the green buttons.
  • Eat a 🍪 cookie to grow longer and score 10 points.
  • Catch a ⭐ star fast for 30 bonus points!
  • Do not bump into the garden fence or your own tail.
