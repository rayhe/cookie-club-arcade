How to Play

  • Your paddle is on the left. Move it with your mouse, your finger, or the up and down arrow keys.
  • Bounce the snowball back. If it slips past Pip, you score a point!
  • Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to send it off at a sneaky angle.
  • Pip gets a tiny bit quicker after every point, but never too fast. First to 5 wins!
